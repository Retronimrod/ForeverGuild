# ForeverGuild v0.9.66

- "Find a group" on the bulletin board is now "Groups & events": a simple event planner for the guild with the tabs Overview, Calendar, My sign-ups, My events and History, a filter bar and a "Create group or event" button.
- Open activities: dungeon, quest group and reputation farming. Raid, recipe run, gear run, material farming, leveling group, world boss and PvP are shown as "Coming Soon" (greyed out, not selectable, not creatable from copies either). Events of earlier categories stay readable and manageable.
- Create wizard in four steps (activity, details, date & participants, summary) with clear states for tiles (selected with gold frame and check, others dimmed, Coming Soon greyed out in its own area) and steps (current in gold, completed with a green check, locked steps grey, missing input marked).
- Dungeon database rebuilt from the original WoW Forever group finder: 34 entries in its order (Upper Blackrock Spire to Ragefire Chasm) with exact German names and level ranges (e.g. 58–60 instead of 55+). Separate entries for Upper/Lower Blackrock Spire, Stratholme Service Entrance/Main Gate, Dire Maul North/West/East and the four Scarlet Monastery wings, each with its own stable ID; renamed: Feste Krol'dok, Schwarzschlundfeste, Terrasse des Formers, Gefängnis von Alcaz, Blackrocktiefen, Halle der Thanen, Ragefireabgrund and more. Unchanged dungeons keep their ID, so existing events stay as they are; events with the former combined dungeons (or the Stockade, which the group finder does not list) stay readable and ask for the wing when edited – no wing is assigned automatically. Wings use the banner of their dungeon complex for now.
- Dungeon choice inside step 2 with search and an optional "Suits my level" filter (other levels stay available), count of shown dungeons, proportional banners and a highlighted selection. The banner itself opens the choice; typed titles and levels are kept, automatically filled values follow a new dungeon.
- Quest group: quest from the quest log or typed. Reputation farming: faction from the character's reputation data (current standing and progress) or typed, shared goal standing Neutral to Exalted.
- Date field with a small calendar, hour 00–23 and minutes 00/15/30/45. No duration for new events: an event that was not started moves to the history six hours after its start as "Expired", never as completed; stored durations of older events are kept.
- Detail view: banner header with status, compact info fields (date, level, zone of the dungeon or goal, group size), organizer and co-organizers with class colors and online status, role columns with confirmed, tentative and free places, waitlist and further groups only when somebody is in them, description and requirements only when filled in. Content scrolls, the action bar stays at the bottom.
- Summary as a finished preview: large dungeon scene in its own proportions, structured blocks for date, time, organizer, goal, group composition, sign-up procedure, description and requirements.
- Participants: group model, place cards per role, own role chosen separately, sign-up procedure, waitlist auto-fill, partner guild visibility and up to three co-organizers from the guild roster.
- Right-click menus in one shared opaque ForeverGuild menu on event cards, calendar days, all tabs, details, participants, waitlist and the "Next event" line of the guild overview. Copy, edit, move, cancel (with confirmation, informs all sign-ups), archive and delete (only without sign-ups, tombstone against re-sync).
- Guild bank redesigned as a compact stock view: header with the vault icon, the guild's name and the last successful capture (with the member who shared it), the guild bank's gold at the top right with the original coin icons, and the existing actions "Scan open vault" and "Request guild snapshot" next to a gear for the settings. The large settings panel on the right is gone; its options are in the central settings.
- Items in compact category blocks side by side, sized by their content and wrapped by the window width (bags, enchanting materials, cloth, herbs, leather, ores & metals, elemental materials, crafting components, recipes, consumables, weapons, armor, miscellaneous; empty ones are hidden). Classic inventory slots with quality borders, stack counts and the original item tooltip plus the amount per bank tab.
- Filter bar with search, category and quality, and "Combine identical items" (display only; items with another enchantment or random bonus stay separate). The vault data and the guild sync are unchanged; the stock can be viewed anywhere, also with the vault closed.
- Guild overview: "Next event: Tomorrow, 19:00 · Stratholme" in its own area right of "Guild members" (date only in narrow windows; title in the tooltip).
- Dungeon icon: a portal instead of the key. Sync with revisions, notifications and reminders without duplicates, recurring dates, migration of the former dungeon dates. Older ForeverGuild versions do not see the new events.

# ForeverGuild v0.9.65

- Fixed: items that bind when picked up (e.g. Gnomish Poultryizer, bracers that bind on pickup) were still offered for crafting orders. The search only had an optional binding filter (default "any") and the order dialog merely showed a hint. Every recipe is now classified by its crafted item: tradeable, not tradeable (bind on pickup, soulbound, account- or quest-bound), unknown (data still loading) or service (enchantments). Only tradeable items and services are listed, counted, sorted or orderable; no option can bypass this, and the "Any binding" filter is gone.
- The decision needs both the structured bind type of the crafted item and the binding line of a private, hidden tooltip for that item. An unloaded tooltip never counts as tradeable; such items are hidden and re-checked when their data arrives. Recipe items themselves are not evaluated, and recipes without a known crafted item only count as services when they are enchantments.
- The order dialog, sending, accepting public orders and accepting direct requests check again; existing orders for items that cannot be handed over are marked "Cannot be handed over" and can still be cancelled or archived. New command /fguild bindcheck <recipe ID> shows the decision (recipe/spell ID, crafted item, bind type, tooltip result, reason).
- Introduction tour: a window manager remembers the open ForeverGuild windows, shows only the window of the current station (character setup alone, main window with the module, settings alone) and restores the previous state at the end, including the settings window when the tour was started there. Menus and tooltips are closed between stations; late callbacks of earlier stations are ignored.
- The information box blocks clicks and the mouse wheel on its whole area, is larger (480 wide, at least 300 high, 24/20 padding, 19/14 pt text, 28 high buttons, 16 high progress bar), scrolls long texts on small screens, keeps clear of the quest tracker and the side navigation and follows moved, resized or rescaled windows without a permanent OnUpdate.
- Concrete highlights: order list on the bulletin board, group list, bank search and inventory, order cards. New station "Find a group" right after the bulletin board and a closing station. Progress is saved by station ID; progress from earlier versions is migrated. All tour texts rewritten in natural German and English.
- New navigation icons from the provided templates: Art/BulletinBoard.tga and Art/GuildBank.tga (256x256, 32 bit, background removed, real transparency). Shared icon references for navigation, help and tour.

# ForeverGuild v0.9.63

- Older crafting orders can be managed again: whisper, mark complete/reopen, withdraw (sent orders that were not delivered yet), create again, archive, restore and remove locally. They are shown in the current layout with requester, crafter, item, quantity, original status, creation date, order ID and materials; payment, material arrangement and handoff are shown as unknown instead of "none".
- Local removal versus cancellation: archiving and removing only affect your own character. Removed orders leave a tombstone, so later, duplicated or delayed sync messages (also from older versions and relays) cannot bring them back. Nobody can delete another player's order for the guild.
- Settings, Data management: archive completed orders, clean up cancelled or expired orders, list older orders and remove or archive a selection, delete archived orders. Every action lists the affected orders first; active orders are never included.
- Fixed: in the order details the item header overlapped "Order overview" for older orders. Sections are now laid out below a header with dynamic height.
- Fixed counters: pending claims count as "Awaiting confirmation", not as handoffs; the navigation counter counts each order once (actions and unread notifications combined).
- The recipe picker for crafting orders now uses the same search as Guild professions: category tree (armor by type and slot), filters, synonyms, crafter list with Place order plus "Post on the bulletin board". Confirmed recipes without a known crafter can be included and are marked "No known crafter".
- Recipe IDs that the client cannot resolve (shown as "#1252979 · Other recipes" before) are hidden by default and counted in the status line; "Show incomplete recipe data" lists them. Arriving item and spell data only refreshes the affected entries.
- Equipment search: armor class filter and folding advanced filters for usability by your character (item tooltip), required level (current level or up to 60), comparison with the equipped item (higher item level, improved selected attributes, higher weighted score) and up to four attribute rows (all or at least one). Active filters are listed above the results.
- Attributes come from the client's item stats (strength, agility, stamina, intellect, spirit, armor, resistances, damage per second and further stats when the client reports them); unknown values are never treated as zero. Free text understands attributes and comparisons, e.g. "mail bracers strength" or "lvl>=55 strength>=10".
- Value column and detail view show the difference to the equipped item (rings, trinkets and one-handers against the weaker equipped item, two-handers against main and off hand), plus the Blizzard comparison tooltip when the client offers it. Optional own attribute weights (Settings, Guild professions) give a weighted score; it is a decision aid, not a simulation.
- New settings window with categories (General, Guild features, Notifications, Data & system, Help & information), search across labels, descriptions and categories with highlighting, scope labels (character, account, guild, guild master only), info tooltips and per-page defaults. Existing settings keep their values. New options: default view when opening and window scale.
- Introduction tour: welcome window on the first start of an account and ten or more stations in the real window with a gold highlight (back, next, skip, end, continue later; progress is saved, combat pauses the tour). Existing users only get a hint. Restart it under Settings, Introduction tour.
- New navigation icons for the guild bank (iron-bound chest with bronze lock) and the bulletin board (notice board with pinned parchments), plus descriptive tooltips for every navigation tab.
- Known limits: weapon speed and bag slot counts are not compared or filtered (no reliable structured client data); partner guilds still do not share crafting orders.

# ForeverGuild v0.9.62

- Crafting marketplace: direct orders and bulletin board orders now share one order model with unique IDs. A board order you claim appears automatically in your order management, and the requester sees it under My orders.
- New order dialog in eight sections: item and recipe (with tooltip), order type and crafter (only members known to craft the recipe, online first), number of crafts, materials per item (what you provide, what the crafter brings, "I provide all/none"), tip or fixed crafting fee in gold/silver/copper (for the whole order or per craft), material reimbursement, handoff method (trade, mail, cash on delivery, custom), message, posting duration and completion deadline. The total is shown before sending.
- Full order lifecycle: request sent, accepted, waiting for materials, in progress, partly crafted, ready for handoff, handoff in progress, completed, plus declined, cancelled, expired, overdue, cancellation requested and clarification needed. Every change is validated against the status and the player's role.
- Payments are documented, never moved: the crafter can propose material costs, the requester confirms or declines; payment is reported by the requester and confirmed by the crafter, or waived. Orders with outstanding agreed payment stay open. ForeverGuild never holds or guarantees gold.
- Changes after acceptance (crafts, fee, handoff, deadline) need the other side's consent; only increases of the fee and longer deadlines apply at once. Cancelling an accepted order requires both sides. Expired postings can be reopened; declined direct orders can be posted on the board or assigned to another crafter.
- Partial work and handoffs: crafting progress, items handed over or sent and items received are tracked with progress bars.
- Order management rebuilt: tabs Incoming orders, My accepted orders, My orders, Ready for handoff and History, counters for new requests, work in progress, handoffs and needed actions, compact order cards and a detail view (overview, participants, crafting, materials, payment, handoff, history) with context-dependent buttons.
- Bulletin board rebuilt as a marketplace: richer cards (payment with coin icons, time left, material arrangement, "you can craft this", taken by another crafter), combinable filters (search, status, profession, category, quality, material arrangement, only craftable, only with payment, minimum payment) and sorting.
- Notifications: pop-up with "Open order", optional sound and chat line, unread counters on the orders tab and the minimap envelope, notification history and one switch per notification type (Settings, Crafting market). Pop-ups wait until combat ends unless enabled. Repeated sync messages never notify twice; reminders for expiring postings, overdue deadlines and pending handoffs.
- Simultaneous claims are resolved by the requester's client; a claim shows "Waiting for confirmation" until it is confirmed or refused.
- Compatibility: older clients still see public orders in the simplified form, orders from older clients keep the simple workflow, existing direct orders stay as they are, and crafters with an older or unknown version receive direct orders over the previous offline-queued path.
- Known limits: partner guilds do not share crafting orders yet; crafting and trades are confirmed manually because WoW events cannot be tied to a specific order reliably. Addon messages travel over the guild channel and are not encrypted; direct orders are only displayed to the two participants.

# ForeverGuild v0.9.61

- New guild-wide recipe search in auction-house style: Guild professions now opens "By recipe" by default ("By crafter" keeps the previous overview; the choice is remembered).
- Collapsible category navigation (weapons, armor by type and slot, jewelry, containers, consumables, trade goods, enchantments by slot, miscellaneous) with result counts; empty categories are greyed out.
- Combinable filters: profession, slot, quality, character level, item level, required skill, crafter, online status and binding, plus "Reset filters".
- Smarter search: several terms are combined and understand common German and English synonyms and abbreviations ("schneider armschienen", "alchi heiltrank", "schmied zweihandschwert"). Unknown terms are reported instead of being ignored.
- Each item appears once with crafter and online counts; recipe variants are merged. The detail view lists variants, materials and all crafters (class, level, skill, status, last sync) with Whisper and Place order, which opens the existing order dialog with item and crafter preselected.
- Only synced recipes count; members without synced data are shown separately and never treated as unable to craft. The search runs on a local index and sends no extra addon messages.

# ForeverGuild v0.9.59

- Roles & permissions: the guild master configures a base profile and three additional roles and assigns them to characters. Moderators can hide board posts and restore them later from the new "Hidden posts" window on the bulletin board. Old moderation records and assignments of former members are cleaned up automatically.
- The guild master can choose which guild ranks count as officers; the permissions window shows whether officers can still publish announcements.
- Partner guilds: guild masters exchange connection codes; the link becomes active once both relays have confirmed each other and can be disconnected by either side. A twink on the same account can act as relay. Unanswered probes back off up to 10 minutes and their "player not online" messages are hidden. Silent partnerships are shown as inactive, all guild members can see the partner list, and a different realm is pointed out.
- Dungeon events can be shared with partner guilds ("Also for partner guilds"). They appear read-only on the partner's board; sign up by whispering the organizer.
- The guild member list shows total and online members, the guild master and officers, and who still runs an older ForeverGuild version.
- New `/fguild partnertest <character>` to check that messages arrive between two characters.
- Internal: UI text now goes through a central localization catalog (Locale.lua).
- The whole guild should update together so all clients respect roles, hidden posts and shared events.
